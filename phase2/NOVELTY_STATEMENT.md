# Phase 2 locked novelty statement

## Defensible statement for Phase 3

Within the frozen 889-record evidence map and the targeted current collision audit, **no study was identified that explicitly treats arterial age as a global non-unique inverse problem by exhaustively testing whether distinct age-conditioned cardiovascular states can generate observationally indistinguishable arterial pulse phenotypes, mapping the physiological compensations that create those cross-age aliases, and quantifying which additional vascular measurements resolve them.**

## Claims explicitly rejected

The project must **not** claim to be:
- the first cardiovascular identifiability study;
- the first cardiovascular sloppiness or measurement-design study;
- the first pulse-wave inverse problem;
- the first multimodal cardiovascular identifiability study;
- the first vascular-age estimator or pulse-derived age study;
- the first arterial system-identification study.

Strong prior art exists for each of those broader claims.

## Novelty lives in the conjunction

The protected novelty is the simultaneous combination of:

1. six known age-conditioned factorial cardiovascular state spaces;
2. exhaustive global cross-age pulse-phenotype non-uniqueness/alias search;
3. explicit recovery of the physiological compensation vectors producing the aliases;
4. measurement-error-conditioned distinguishability rather than classifier performance alone;
5. prespecified measurement-rescue analysis asking which added signals/sites break the aliases;
6. a conventional vascular-age benchmark and local information-geometric explanation as subordinate analyses.

If any future source executes this conjunction materially before manuscript submission, Phase 3 must reassess the novelty claim.

# Phase 2 limitations

1. **Original Scopus query unavailable.** The exact query that produced the frozen 889 records was not preserved. The corpus is hash-reproducible; the database search is not exactly reproducible.
2. **Title/abstract-level frozen-corpus adjudication.** The 111-record collision audit is based on titles and abstracts available in the export. It does not masquerade as full-text review.
3. **Single reviewer/agent.** No duplicate independent screening or inter-rater agreement statistic is claimed.
4. **Corpus retrieval history.** The 889-record search was partly optimized around a prior Mori–Zwanzig arterial-memory question. A targeted current collision audit was therefore added for cardiovascular identifiability, pulse-wave inverse problems, and vascular-age measurement design.
5. **No meta-analysis.** There is no common quantitative effect size across the heterogeneous methodological and ageing literature; pooling would be scientifically misleading.
6. **Absence claims are bounded.** The proper conclusion is “no direct collision was identified in the mapped corpus and targeted collision audit,” not “no prior study exists.”
7. **External collision audit is targeted rather than exhaustive.** It is designed to test the most dangerous novelty claims, not to replace a newly executed multi-database systematic review.

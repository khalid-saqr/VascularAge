# Phase 3 Novelty Boundary

Phase 3 adopts the Phase-2 evidence boundary unchanged.

## Permitted novelty statement

Within the frozen 889-record evidence map and the targeted current collision audit, **no study was identified that explicitly treats arterial age as a global non-unique inverse problem by exhaustively testing whether distinct age-conditioned cardiovascular states can generate observationally indistinguishable arterial pulse phenotypes, mapping the physiological compensations that create those cross-age aliases, and quantifying which additional vascular measurements resolve them.**

## Prohibited claims

The project must not claim to be:
- the first cardiovascular identifiability study;
- the first cardiovascular sloppiness or measurement-design study;
- the first pulse-wave inverse problem;
- the first multimodal cardiovascular identifiability study;
- the first vascular-age estimator or pulse-derived age study;
- the first arterial system-identification study.

Novelty resides in the conjunction of the six age-conditioned factorial state spaces, exhaustive cross-age aliasing, mechanistic compensation mapping, explicit measurement-error-conditioned distinguishability, and prespecified measurement rescue.

If materially colliding work appears before manuscript submission, the novelty statement must be reassessed without changing the locked biological estimands.
